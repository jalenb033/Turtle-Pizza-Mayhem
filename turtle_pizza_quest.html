    How to play, save, and open

    Save this page’s complete HTML as turtle_pizza_quest.html, choose “All files” if using a text editor, then double-click the file to open it in a modern web browser. It works offline.

    Use arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen buttons. Press P or the Pause button for a break. Switching tabs pauses play. Your goal is to collect pizza and complete all eight learning checkpoints. Each checkpoint opens after 10 seconds of active play, even if you have not collected pizza. Three slices or touching the flag lights it early, but never shortens the 10-second interval. Answer with a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter.

    All timers, monsters, and movement pause during questions and feedback. There is no answer countdown. An incorrect answer earns no bonus and includes an explanation; it never costs health. When health reaches zero, the turtle returns to camp; your answers, pizza score, and remaining time are kept. After question 8, you receive your final score and answer review.
